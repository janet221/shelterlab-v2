# 01 Vision

ShelterLab turns animal shelters into safe, non-contact natural-science Living Laboratories. Government open data becomes curriculum evidence; teacher-governed learning qualifies students through a Research License; structured observations become shelter-confirmed evidence; and only approved evidence may later support adoption services and aggregate impact reporting.

### VIS-001 Evidence-driven One Health platform

- **Description:** ShelterLab shall connect education, animal welfare, environmental context, public health, citizen science, and policy through one auditable product journey.
- **Priority:** Must
- **Delivery state:** Partial
- **User story:** As a competition judge, I want to trace public evidence into learning and shelter value so that innovation is demonstrable rather than asserted.
- **Acceptance criteria:** A trace identifies source, curriculum/resource mapping, governed question, license result, observation review, and any downstream approved use; unavailable future links are labeled.
- **Related modules:** Open Data, Curriculum, Research License, Observation, Evidence Chain, Adoption Accelerator, Analytics
- **Competition value:** Core innovation, educational value, social impact, and feasibility narrative.
- **Government-data dependency:** Required for public-evidence context and EDUOD positioning.
- **AI dependency:** Optional; deterministic/manual paths remain valid.
- **Evidence chain dependency:** Required end to end.

### VIS-002 Human authority by domain

- **Description:** Teachers shall govern education decisions, shelter staff shall govern animal/public evidence, and AI shall never approve, publish, diagnose, or decide eligibility.
- **Priority:** Must
- **Delivery state:** Partial
- **User story:** As a teacher or shelter authority, I want domain decisions to remain human-owned so that the platform is safe and accountable.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Backend transitions reject skipped reviews, teacher shelter-confirmation, AI approval, automatic publication, and unauthorized profile decisions.
- **Related modules:** Authorization, AI Governance, Question Authoring, Observation Review, Adoption Accelerator
- **Competition value:** Responsible AI and credible cross-organization governance.
- **Government-data dependency:** None.
- **AI dependency:** Governance applies whenever AI is enabled.
- **Evidence chain dependency:** Human review actions are separate evidence nodes.

### VIS-003 Honest impact claims

- **Description:** ShelterLab shall distinguish measured, pilot, verified-fixture, demo-reference, and synthetic evidence and shall not imply causality without an approved evaluation design.
- **Priority:** Must
- **Delivery state:** Partial
- **User story:** As a judge or public stakeholder, I want every claim labeled by evidence quality so that I can trust the demonstration.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Dashboards label data mode and provenance; synthetic metrics are not presented as real outcomes; unsupported causal language is blocked or flagged.
- **Related modules:** Dashboard, Analytics, Open Data, Competition Demo
- **Competition value:** Trustworthiness and technical rigor.
- **Government-data dependency:** Provenance labels required where government data is used.
- **AI dependency:** AI-generated claims require the same labels and review.
- **Evidence chain dependency:** Claim must resolve to a source/snapshot and metric definition.
