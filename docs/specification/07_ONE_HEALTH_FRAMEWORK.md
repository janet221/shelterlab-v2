# 07 One Health Framework

### OHF-001 Multi-domain inquiry framing

- **Description:** One Health inquiry shall connect human health, animal welfare, environment, education, citizen science, and policy without collapsing them into diagnosis.
- **Priority:** Must
- **Delivery state:** Implemented deterministic synthetic MVP in Sprint 9A
- **User story:** As a science teacher, I want students to investigate connected systems so that shelter learning exceeds simple animal observation.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Each inquiry names at least two domains, identifies evidence and limitations, and avoids clinical or causal claims unsupported by design.
- **Related modules:** Curriculum, Inquiry Coach, Open Data, Observation, Reporting
- **Competition value:** Educational innovation and social relevance.
- **Government-data dependency:** Approved aggregate sources provide context.
- **AI dependency:** Optional inquiry coaching only.
- **Evidence chain dependency:** Inquiry claims cite source and observation evidence.

### OHF-002 Scientific inquiry cycle

- **Description:** The learning journey shall support question formation, source review, variable definition, safe data collection, analysis, limitation review, communication, and reflection.
- **Priority:** Must
- **Delivery state:** Implemented deterministic synthetic MVP in Sprint 9A; pilot validation pending
- **User story:** As a student, I want a repeatable inquiry cycle so that I learn scientific reasoning rather than only data entry.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Teacher-approved inquiry templates capture question, variables, method, evidence, limitations, and conclusion; assessment rubric records outcomes.
- **Related modules:** 16-Week Course, Living Lab, Learning Evidence
- **Competition value:** Demonstrable pedagogy.
- **Government-data dependency:** Used in source review/comparison where available.
- **AI dependency:** Optional feedback; never authors final conclusion automatically.
- **Evidence chain dependency:** Inquiry artifact links sources, sessions, and rubric.

### OHF-003 Welfare and public-health boundaries

- **Description:** One Health content shall teach hygiene, zoonotic-risk awareness, welfare, and prevention without allowing students or AI to diagnose animals or people.
- **Priority:** Must
- **Delivery state:** Implemented in curriculum rules
- **User story:** As shelter authority, I want prevention-focused education so that field activity does not create unsafe medical claims.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Course and quiz content distinguishes awareness/prevention from diagnosis; incident routes direct students to staff; prohibited claims are flagged.
- **Related modules:** Research License, Safety, AI Governance, Observation
- **Competition value:** Responsible health education.
- **Government-data dependency:** Public-health sources require verification before use.
- **AI dependency:** Diagnostic output prohibited.
- **Evidence chain dependency:** Safety completion and incidents are traceable.

### OHF-004 Policy communication

- **Description:** Student reports may produce limited policy or service recommendations only after teacher review and privacy-safe aggregation.
- **Priority:** Should
- **Delivery state:** Implemented bounded synthetic workflow in Sprint 9A; local-government pilot pending
- **User story:** As a local-government viewer, I want evidence-limited recommendations so that citizen science can inform discussion responsibly.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Reports state data mode, sample limits, uncertainty, and no individual identities; teacher approval is recorded before sharing.
- **Related modules:** Scientific Reporting, Analytics, Government Dashboard
- **Competition value:** Civic impact and policy relevance.
- **Government-data dependency:** Required where policy context cites official statistics.
- **AI dependency:** AI may draft summaries but cannot publish.
- **Evidence chain dependency:** Report claims resolve to approved evidence and review.
